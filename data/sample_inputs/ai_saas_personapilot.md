# Sample stimulus — product concept (target: us_smb)

PersonaPilot is an AI copilot for product managers that turns customer
interview transcripts, support tickets, and NPS comments into structured,
always-current user personas — no more stale persona slides from 18 months
ago. Upload a transcript or connect Zendesk/Intercom, and PersonaPilot's AI
extracts goals, objections, and jobs-to-be-done, then auto-updates the
relevant persona in real time. Every persona links back to the exact quotes
that justify it, so nobody has to take the AI's word for it.

Pricing: Starter is $49/month for 1 workspace, 3 personas, and 200 processed
conversations/month. Team is $199/month for 10 personas, unlimited
conversations, Slack alerts when a persona shifts, and CSV/Notion export.
Enterprise is custom pricing with SSO, on-prem data residency, and a
dedicated success manager. 14-day free trial on Starter and Team, no credit
card required.

Used by 260 product teams; teams report cutting persona-refresh time from 2
days a quarter to under an hour. SOC2 Type II in progress, data never used to
train shared models.
