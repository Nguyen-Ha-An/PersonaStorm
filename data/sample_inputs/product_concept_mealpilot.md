# Sample stimulus — product concept (target: parents)

MealPilot is an AI meal planning app for busy families. It builds a weekly
dinner plan around your kids' allergies, auto-generates a grocery list, and
syncs with Instacart. Pricing: $12/month after a 14-day free trial. Cancel
anytime. Used by 4,000 families; average family saves 3 hours a week.
