> ⛈ The product wind tunnel — 1,000 calibrated synthetic personas storm-test
> your product concept and deliver one honest, system-computed market-fit

## Long description (≤100 words)

> PersonaStorm is a product wind tunnel: paste a concept, landing page, ad, or
> pricing table, and 1,000 evidence-backed synthetic personas evaluate it
> across 17 calibrated market criteria, streaming live as a green/yellow/red
> swarm. Every number — including the market-fit score — is computed by a
> deterministic scoring engine, never invented by an LLM, and a
> trust/calibration panel tells you exactly how much to believe each run.
> Built as a full SaaS (Next.js, Supabase auth, credit billing, admin console)
> with Fireworks AI inference, an offline mock mode, and 421 passing tests, it
> delivers directional market research in about a minute, for cents.
